# Channels Feature - User Guide

## What are Channels?

Channels in Meowsenger are similar to Telegram channels - they allow you to broadcast messages to many subscribers. Only channel admins can post messages, while all subscribers can read them.

## Creating a Channel

1. Navigate to the Channels page by clicking the megaphone/campaign icon in the bottom menu
2. Click "New Channel" button
3. Fill in the channel details:
   - **Channel Name**: Required, 3-20 characters
   - **Description**: Optional
   - **Public/Private**: Toggle whether the channel is public or private
4. Click "OK" to create

You will automatically become the owner/admin of the channel.

## Using Channels

### As a Subscriber

When you subscribe to a channel, you can:
- View all channel posts
- Reply to posts (if you're an admin)
- Forward channel messages to chats
- Leave the channel at any time

To leave a channel:
1. Open the channel
2. Click the menu icon (top center)
3. Click "Leave Channel"

### As a Channel Admin

As an admin, you can:
- Post messages to the channel
- Reply to messages
- Forward messages
- Delete your own messages
- View the subscriber list
- Access channel settings

To post a message:
1. Open your channel
2. Type your message in the input box at the bottom
3. Press Enter or click Send

To access settings:
1. Open your channel
2. Click the menu icon (top center)
3. Click "Settings"

### As a Channel Owner

As the channel owner (first admin), you can do everything an admin can do, plus:
- Add new admins
- Remove admins
- Change channel name and description

To add an admin:
1. Open your channel
2. Click the menu icon (top center)
3. Click "Subscribers"
4. Find the user you want to promote
5. Click the promote button next to their name

## Channel Types

### Public Channels
- Can be discovered and joined by any user (once discovery is implemented)
- Anyone can see the channel exists

### Private Channels
- Can only be joined via invite link (coming soon) or being added by an admin
- Not visible in public channel lists

## Channels vs Groups

| Feature | Channels | Groups |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Who can post | Only admins | All members |
| Purpose | Broadcasting | Discussion |
| Member count | Unlimited subscribers | Active participants |
| Prefix in list | "ch." | "g." |

## Tips

1. **Choose descriptive names**: Make it easy for subscribers to identify your channel
2. **Use descriptions**: Explain what your channel is about
3. **Post regularly**: Keep subscribers engaged with regular updates
4. **Promote carefully**: Only give admin rights to trusted users
5. **Monitor activity**: Check your subscriber list regularly

## Limitations

Current limitations (may be addressed in future updates):
- No channel deletion UI (contact admin)
- No invite links for private channels yet
- No channel discovery/search yet
- Cannot remove subscribers (they must leave voluntarily)
- No reactions or comments on posts
- No post editing

## Troubleshooting

**Can't post to a channel?**
- Make sure you're an admin of the channel
- Check if you're still subscribed to the channel

**Channel not appearing in list?**
- Make sure you're subscribed
- Refresh the page
- Check that you're on the Channels page (not Chats)

**Can't see the Channels button?**
- Make sure you're logged in
- The channels button (megaphone icon) appears in the bottom menu next to the chat icon
